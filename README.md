# Ages of Siege

An audio-first tower defense game. Man the guns atop your walls and hold back the siege by ear, from a medieval castle to a modern base.

Play in the browser (headphones recommended): https://christopherabel3000-creator.github.io/Ages-of-Siege/

- Phone: swipe left or right to aim, tap to fire, swipe up for status, swipe down to repeat, two-finger tap to pause.
- Keyboard: arrows to aim, 1 to 5 to jump to a lane, F or Space to fire, S for status, R to repeat, Escape to pause.

See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the full design.
